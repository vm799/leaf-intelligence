Complete Clinical Trial Analysis

Trial Portfolio Overview

The combined clinical trial portfolio encompasses 15+ studies with 2,340+ patients enrolled across all phases of development. Trial designs range from traditional randomized controlled trials (Vepdegestrant VERITAC-2, Atirmociclib FourLight-3) to single-arm dose escalation studies (177Lu-NeoB combinations). Geographic distribution includes global trials (Atirmociclib), China-focused development (Disitamab), and US/EU concentration (Vepdegestrant). Patient populations span first-line (Atirmociclib) through heavily pretreated (Vepdegestrant median 3 prior lines) settings.
