Research Methodology

Pipeline Evaluation Framework

This analysis employs a phase-stratified evaluation framework designed to assess oncology assets with heightened scrutiny for data quality, regional variations, and regulatory alignment. The methodology emphasizes critical evaluation of uncontrolled early-phase data, particularly from regions with documented reporting disparities.

Key Evaluation Criteria

  • Control Arm Validity: Assessment of comparator appropriateness, with preference for placebo-controlled designs over heterogeneous standard-of-care comparators
  • Regional Data Quality: Enhanced scrutiny of China-originated data with corroboration requirements from US/EU studies
  • Adverse Event Capture: Evaluation of AE elicitation methods, distinguishing between investigator-observed and patient-reported outcomes
  • Biomarker Validation: Requirements for control arms in enrichment designs to validate predictive biomarkers
  • Probability of Technical & Regulatory Success (PTRS): Integration of AI-generated assessments with subject matter expert validation

Data Sources & Verification

Primary data sourced from ClinicalTrials.gov, regulatory filings, investigator brochures, and company disclosures. All Phase 1b uncontrolled data, particularly from Asia-Pacific regions, underwent additional verification requirements including identification of US/EU bridging studies.
